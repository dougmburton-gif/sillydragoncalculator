How to play

Click or tap anywhere near the flying dragon to throw your rope. A hit snares the dragon in a glowing orange square — you then have 4 seconds (watch the pulsing timer, top-left) to drag it into the corral in the bottom-right corner. Get it there in time and you earn points; run out of time and it breaks free, and you'll see a "bad old dragon" banner fly by. Every 5 points takes you up a level, and the dragon resets a little faster each time. From level 3 onward, the dragon can turn around and breathe fire at you — if it catches you, that's game over (a happy one — nobody actually gets hurt around here). Your best score is saved in this browser.

Every 5 seconds, a glowing number (1–5) floats onto the field — grab it to power up your rope. Higher numbers give your rope a bigger, easier-to-land catch radius, a brighter glow, and more bonus points per catch — but the power fades over time (a "1" only lasts 3 seconds; a "5" lasts 15). Time it well and chain power-ups to keep your rope glowing.

Every so often the dragon broadcasts a burst of chocolate chips. A warning triangle (with a number 1–5) appears exactly 1 second before the broadcast — rope it in time and a "chip diaper" shield appears under the dragon for that many seconds, sweeping the chips safely off both sides of the screen. Miss it, and getting hit costs a flat 5 calories per bombardment (no matter how many chips land) and makes you grow 5% bigger — reach 50 calories and it's game over. The moment an incoming chip gets within a centimeter of you, your face turns dark brown with red eyes and a frown (plus a chocolate smear) for 1 second before you go back to smiling. If the dragon ever flies within 2 inches of your head, it drops chips immediately — no warning that time. The sky also shifts through 7 moods as you level up, from a calm morning to a stormy dusk, with the dragon getting faster each time.
