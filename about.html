🐲 About Silly Dragon Calculator

Who We Are

Silly Dragon Calculator is a small, independently-run website. It's built and maintained by a single site owner, not a company or agency — the goal from day one has been to make something genuinely useful and a little bit fun, not just another generic calculator page. I'm also the person behind the site's blog posts about archery, coaching youth volleyball and basketball, and a few of the other "from the owner" pieces you'll find linked around the site — this isn't a faceless content operation, it's one person's actual hobby project that grew into something people use daily.

Why a Dragon, and Why So Many Colors

The dragon theme started as a way to make basic arithmetic feel less like a chore, especially for kids working through homework. Each calculator got its own dragon and color early on (yellow for basic, green for mortgage, navy for scientific, silver for graphing) mostly because it made the site easier to navigate at a glance, and it turned out people — both kids and adults doing quick math — genuinely responded to it. What started as a small visual gimmick is now the thing most returning visitors mention first.

What We Offer

The site started as a set of free browser-based calculators (basic, scientific, graphing, and mortgage) with a playful dragon theme. It's since grown to include word games, a hobby farm and dog breed directory, a free homeschool curriculum guide, and a few "just for fun" extras like the daily horoscope and lucky numbers pages. Everything runs directly in your browser — nothing you type into a calculator is ever sent to a server or stored, which also means the calculators work fine with a spotty connection or offline once the page has loaded.

How the Calculators Are Built and Checked

Each calculator uses standard, well-established formulas — the same order-of-operations rules and amortization math you'd find in a textbook or financial reference, not a proprietary black-box algorithm. When we add a new calculator or fix a reported bug, we test it against known correct answers (a mortgage payment we can verify by hand, a trig identity with a known result) before it goes live. If you ever spot a calculation that looks wrong, the Contact page is the fastest way to get it looked at — bug reports get real attention here, not a form-letter reply.

How the Site Stays Free

Silly Dragon Calculator is supported by advertising and by affiliate links to products related to the topic at hand (for example, on the Hobby Farm and Dog Breeds pages, and in a few blog posts about gear I actually use). If you click one of those links and make a purchase, the site may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
